* <2025-04-05 Fri>
** Touching... feeling... holding...
- The inventory system has been overhauled, and is now nearly
  identical to IN3's inventory. Just press F3!
  - The crazy part is that we did it by repurposing the DarkRP pocket
    instead of making a whole new inventory from scratch. This little
    shortcut added a few years back to all of our lives.
- As this inventory piggybacks off the DarkRP pocket, pressing ~KEY_E~
  on an item in-world causes it to be used instead of picked up.
* <2025-04-04 Sat>
** It stares back
- Added a new death +screen+ screamer.
** Nightmare Vision
- Added goggles/gasmask filters.
  - Applied these filters to Sanitar and Kommandant.
    - ... and some color fuckery, for good measure.
  - In the future, this will be tied to items rather than fates.
** I can hear them chew...!
- Added action text for eating foods.
** The bones are still soft
- Added live babies, for when you want your meal to suffer.
- More nutritious than abortions, for those of rich blood.
* <2025-04-02 Sat>
** The middle man
- Replaced sawblades with spawn portals, which turn ghosts into living
  fates when they touch them.
* <2025-04-01 Tue>
** Let me out!
- Added Corpse-Cam, to watch your cadaver after death.
- You must wait for your corpse to rot before respawning.
- Players now respawn as ghosts instead of their current fate.
* <2025-03-27 Thu>
** Rise from your grave!
- You can now meet your mortal fate by cutting yourself on sawblades
  throughout the map, much like the old game. This is a temporary
  solution and will be succeeded by the use of computer terminals.
- Added third-person view to ghosts.
- Ghosts no longer suffer from fall damage.
** Only darkness for you
Say goodbye to your flashlights.
** No free bones
Salaries have been disabled, so you have to work for a living.
** Mmm... Lunch...
- Added slop skulls. Eating it sates your emptiness. Satisfactory.
- Added abortions. Eating it sates your emptiness, wets your thirst,
  and refills your blood.
- Added rib bones, for those desperate enough to break their teeth
  cracking them open.
- Added paper, the first "vegan" food. Remember to wash it down.
* <2025-03-26 Wed>
** Goodbye, scum
Miscreants and Kingpin are removed from the game, because they are
more-or-less just DarkRP gangsters.

When antagonism starts to be focused on, they will probably come back.
** Oh boy!
Map =rp_genova_redux_v1= was given spots on the map for boulders to
spawn and will probably see actual use in-game.

At the time of writing this, there are plans to consult with a mapper
for EvilRP-specific matters, but this is not currently happening.
** My lovely immersion
To compensate for entire removal of voice chat, sounds that play
whenever you send a message according to your specific player class
have been re-implemented and works pretty much exactly like the old
game.

The sounds used for this are the same as from +the old country+
Industrial Nightmare 3. When players least expect it, the sounds will
be changed to something unfamiliar to them.
** Scrub 'til it sparkles
=weapon_physcannon= and =gmod_camera= weapons were removed from default
weapons. Eventually =keys= and =pocket= will go too -- =weapon_physgun= and
=gmod_tool=, if they cannot be functionally replaced, will be reskinned
into the Telegland/Hexengland as in Nightmare 3.
** Straying from the path
Against all warnings plastered all over DarkRP, we have made the
regrettable decision to alter its base files somewhat.

So far, we've used this to achieve the following:
- Loss of official support for DarkRP related matters.

  Oops.
- Global notifications when somebody's job changes were removed.

  Of course, whenever somebody goes Kommandant or something, everyone
  will know.
- Auto-refresh works thanks to disabling Simplerr.

  Simplerr is a debug assistant for DarkRP. I am confident enough in
  my ability to read gLua errors that I feel I do not need it, and the
  loss of auto-refresh is a terrible problem to have -- worse than no debugger.
** G-g-g-ghosts...
Ghosts now exist. They are the default class one has when they first
join the server. Like in +the old country+ Nightmare 3, they see reduced
colors (except red), hear differently, cannot be seen by the living,
do not collide with most things, _and can fly_.

* <2025-03-25 Tue>
** The bitch that is metabolism
Victims of EvilRP now experience hunger. It accumulates at the same
rate that it used to in +the old country+ Industrial Nightmare 3, and is
called =emptiness= just the same.
* <2025-03-22 Sat>
** Uh oh
Walking and running speed was reduced to more closely match the
original Nightmare 3.
** Jumpscares
I forgot to mention with the disabling of voice chat that jumpscares
were re-implemented.
** Lootables
Objects you can use to dispense random junk items have been
implemented in EvilRP; we just now need to give them junk items to
actually drop. Probably it will start with food and scrap metal
materials.
** Rifle changes
The Livermann was renamed to the Enforcer.
The Enforcer and Subjugator were given their original sounds again.
The Enforcer reloads two rounds per load instead of one, now.
* <2025-03-21 Fri>
** RIP JFK
Killing someone with a shot to the head is now a mindblowing act.

In classic Nightmare 3, the head would simply be shrunk until no
longer visible to give the illusion of the head disappearing.

In EvilRP, the head is changed to a bodygroup which takes the
appearance of an actual severed head stump.

Blood gushing from the neck's stump will be implemented later after
more important things.
** L.R.D.
The Medic's medkit is now named the =L.R.D.= and makes a different noise
more like the one from the classic Nightmare 3. It does not have the
same functionality as the Korpsman's Lovebox yet, but it will.
** The truth
+Hobos+ Unfortunate people now say weird, unhinged things when
right-clicking with the Bugbait.
** I HAVE NO MOUTH AND I MUST SCREAM
Voice chat is now gone from EvilRP. Probably forever. Wee!

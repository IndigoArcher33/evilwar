* Jobs
Beacon & crowd system, elected/appointed jobs that voluntarily hire workers *at their own expense*.
This creates a lumpenproletariat that needs money to live but can't be employed.
*Beacons*
- Kommandant
  Rules the city. Appoints beacon jobs.
- Kapitan
  Oversees the Sanitar Squad. Can hire Sanitars.
- Foreman
  Oversees the factories. Can hire Workers.
- Physician
  The chief doctor of the city. Can hire Korpsmen.

* PLEASE READ ME FIRST
Maybe you're not a member of our team.  In that case, welcome!  You
deserve an explanation of some things before you take this document's
name and intentions too seriously.

By /plunder/ we're referring to code that is already public, and open
source/freely available for anyone to use and redistribute.

If you believe we are using stolen code or otherwise infringing on the
rights of any other programmer(s), please contact Mukhomorinka
immediately with your claims.
